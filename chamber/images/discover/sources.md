## Factual notes

- Keizer Station uses the verified Target anchor address as a visitor reference, not a single address for the entire shopping center. Its former keizerstation.com domain is parked; do not use it for Learn More.
- The stadium operator currently uses Roto-Rooter Park. The accepted Volcanoes Stadium name is retained with that clarification.
- The museum photograph depicts its Heritage Center building, not an interior exhibit.
- The garden photograph is an iris close-up taken on site, not a panoramic view. The official homepage footer gives ZIP 97303; the bloom page location block conflicts with 97393. Use the homepage/customer-service address.
- Schreiner’s is near Keizer, outside the city. The Capitol and Willamette Heritage Center are in Salem.
- The legislature homepage reported the building closed for construction during research; no interior access or tour hours are promised.
- Photos are historical views, not evidence of current access conditions.
- Commons image metadata confirmed the named photographers and licenses. The Flickr page explicitly grants CC BY 2.0 and its caption identifies the gardens as the photo location.
- Original Commons downloads were used except for the Capitol, where HTTP 429 required the same photograph’s 960px Commons thumbnail. Query parameters used for download retries do not change the source image.
